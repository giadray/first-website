Your allergies and preferences, set once.

Tell PlateSafe what matters to you, and every listing you see reflects it — automatically, before you've even started searching.

Once it's set, it's applied automatically every time you search — no more re-explaining your allergies to a new server every single visit.

List exact allergens and intolerances, not just broad categories — peanuts and tree nuts are flagged separately, for example.

Save preferences for the whole household — useful if you're managing allergies for kids or family members too.
